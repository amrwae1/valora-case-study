# CHAPTER 01

## Eyebrow

The Challenge

---

## Heading

Sales managers don't have a data problem.

They have a decision problem.

---

## Body

Modern sales organizations generate more performance data than ever before.

CRM activity, conversation intelligence, QA reviews, and revenue metrics provide unprecedented visibility into sales performance.

Yet managers still struggle to identify what deserves attention, understand why performance changes happen, and translate insights into coaching that improves future outcomes.

The problem isn't collecting information.

The problem is transforming information into confident coaching decisions.

## Section Title

### Principle 01

Evidence before recommendation

Every recommendation explains why it exists before suggesting action.

---

### Principle 02

Human judgment stays in control

Managers review, adjust, and approve recommendations before coaching begins.

---

### Principle 03

Coaching is collaborative

Managers and representatives work toward shared goals with transparency and measurable progress.
