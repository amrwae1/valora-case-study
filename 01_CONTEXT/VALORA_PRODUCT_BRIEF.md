# VALORA PRODUCT BRIEF

## Product

Valora

## Category

Enterprise Performance Intelligence Platform

## One-Sentence Description

Valora helps sales leaders turn performance signals into coaching that drives measurable growth.

---

## The Problem

Sales organizations already have CRM systems, conversation intelligence platforms, QA tools, and dashboards.

The challenge isn't collecting data.

The challenge is knowing what deserves attention, understanding why performance changes happen, and turning insights into coaching that improves future outcomes.

---

## The Solution

Valora transforms performance signals into evidence-based coaching recommendations.

Managers review AI-generated insights, validate supporting evidence, adjust recommendations when needed, and guide representatives through structured coaching plans.

The platform keeps humans in control while using AI to accelerate understanding.

---

## Primary Users

- Sales Managers
- Sales Directors

---

## Core Principles

- Evidence before recommendation
- Human judgment stays in control
- Coaching is collaborative
- Continuous learning
