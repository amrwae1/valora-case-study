# Product Identity

## Name

Valora

## Category

Performance Intelligence Platform

## Tagline

Turn performance signals into coaching that drives measurable growth.

---

# Product Overview

Valora is a conceptual enterprise platform that helps sales leaders transform performance signals into structured coaching decisions.

Instead of functioning as another analytics dashboard, Valora connects CRM activity, conversation intelligence, QA reviews, and coaching history into one intelligent coaching workflow.

Its purpose is to reduce decision ambiguity, increase coaching quality, and create measurable improvements in sales performance.

---

# Mission

Help sales leaders make better coaching decisions through evidence, transparency, and continuous learning.

---

# Vision

Create a coaching platform where AI accelerates understanding while managers remain responsible for decisions.

Performance data should become actionable coaching—not more dashboards.

# Product Philosophy

## Principle 01

Evidence before Recommendation

Every AI recommendation must explain:

- Why it exists
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence level
- Business impact

AI never asks users to trust conclusions blindly.

---

## Principle 02

Human Judgment Stays in Control

AI accelerates decisions.

Managers remain responsible for decisions.

Recommendations should always be:

- Reviewable
- Editable
- Rejectable

---

## Principle 03

Coaching is Collaborative

Managers and representatives work together toward measurable growth.

The experience encourages:

- Transparency
- Practice
- Feedback
- Accountability
- Progress

Never surveillance.

Never punishment.

---

## Principle 04

Continuous Learning

Every coaching outcome improves future recommendations.

The platform continuously learns from:

- Coaching outcomes
- Manager decisions
- Representative progress
- Team behavior

# Interaction Philosophy

Every interaction should answer a question.

Hover

Can I interact?

Click

Did the system understand me?

Loading

What is happening?

Result

Why did this happen?

Next

What should I do now?

# AI Experience Guidelines

AI should never feel mysterious.

Whenever AI produces a recommendation, users should understand:

- What data was analyzed
- Why the recommendation exists
- Confidence level
- Supporting evidence
- Ability to modify or reject

Transparency builds trust.

# Prompting Guidelines

Whenever using AI tools (Framer, Stitch, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.):

- Treat Valora as a real enterprise product.
- Prioritize storytelling over marketing.
- Prioritize clarity over decoration.
- Generate layouts before details.
- Use editorial composition.
- Keep generous whitespace.
- Avoid generic SaaS visuals.
- Avoid AI clichés.
- Design every section around a single message.
